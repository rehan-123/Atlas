# Microsoft Authenticator

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Microsoft's phone app for approving sign-ins, showing codes and signing in without a password.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Identity tools hold user accounts and decide who can sign in to what. When identity breaks, nobody can work.

**Made by:** [[Microsoft]] · **Category:** [[Identity & Access]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **authenticator app**: Phone app (such as Microsoft Authenticator) that approves sign-ins or shows one-time codes for MFA.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Authenticator app for push notifications with number matching, TOTP codes, passkeys and passwordless phone sign-in.
