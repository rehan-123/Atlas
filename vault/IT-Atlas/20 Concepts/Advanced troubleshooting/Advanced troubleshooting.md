# Advanced troubleshooting

Deep troubleshooting techniques for problems the playbooks don't solve.

Part of [[Concepts]].

- [[Packet capture]]: Recording the actual network traffic to see what's really happening, instead of guessing.
- [[Wireshark filters]]: The display filters that turn a huge capture into the few packets that matter.
- [[Process Monitor tracing]]: Watching every file, registry and process action an app makes, to find what it's failing on.
- [[Performance analysis]]: Measuring where a slow system actually spends its time: CPU, memory, disk or network.
- [[Crash dump analysis]]: Reading a memory dump after a blue screen or app crash to find the driver or module responsible.
- [[Kerberos troubleshooting]]: Finding why Kerberos sign-ins fail: time, names (SPNs), tickets and encryption types.
- [[Group Policy troubleshooting]]: Working out why a policy isn't applying: scope, filtering, replication and client processing.
- [[Certificate chain troubleshooting]]: Fixing 'certificate not trusted' errors by checking the whole chain from the server certificate up to a trusted root.
- [[TLS handshake troubleshooting]]: Diagnosing failures when a client and server can't agree on a secure connection.
- [[Directory sync troubleshooting]]: Fixing objects that won't sync between Active Directory and Entra ID.
- [[SMB signing and versions]]: Why SMB version and signing settings break file sharing between old and new devices.
- [[Divide and conquer troubleshooting]]: Cutting a big problem in half again and again until the cause is cornered.
- [[Event log correlation]]: Lining up events from several logs and systems by time to find the real cause rather than the loudest symptom.
