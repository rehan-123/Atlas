# User accounts and permissions

> [!abstract] In one line
> Who you are (account) and what you're allowed to open or change (permissions).

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A staff badge (account) and the doors it opens (permissions). Teams get the same badge type (group).

## How it works

- Your account proves who you are when you sign in.
- Groups bundle people who need the same access (for example 'Finance team').
- Permissions are given to groups on folders, mailboxes and apps; add the person to the group rather than to each folder.
- **Admin** accounts can change the system. Day-to-day work should use a normal account (least privilege).

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Why add users to groups instead of giving permission to each person?
> It's easier to manage and audit: change the group once and everyone in it gets the right access.
