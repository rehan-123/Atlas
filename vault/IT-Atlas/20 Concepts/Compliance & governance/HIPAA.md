# HIPAA

> [!abstract] In one line
> US law protecting health information; any IT provider handling patient data for a clinic must follow its Security Rule.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Doctor-patient confidentiality, extended to everyone who handles the records, including IT.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MSP**: Managed service provider: a company that runs IT for other companies.
> **BAA**: Business Associate Agreement: the HIPAA contract required before a provider handles patient data.
> **PHI**: Protected health information: patient data covered by HIPAA.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Applies to covered entities (clinics, insurers) and their **business associates** (including MSPs).
- A **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** is required before handling protected health information (PHI).
- Security Rule safeguards: access control, audit logs, encryption, backups, risk assessments, training.
- Breaches must be reported; fines can be large.
