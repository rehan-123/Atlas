# Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Delivers Windows desktops and apps from the data center to any device.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** VDI gives users a desktop that actually runs in the data center or cloud, which they reach over the network.

**Made by:** [[Cloud Software Group]] · **Runs:** On-prem or Citrix DaaS · **Category:** [[Virtualization & Storage]] › [[VDI & app delivery]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **VDI**: Virtual desktop infrastructure: desktops that run on servers and are used remotely.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Citrix's app and desktop virtualization: Delivery Controllers, StoreFront, VDAs on session hosts, and NetScaler Gateway for remote access.
