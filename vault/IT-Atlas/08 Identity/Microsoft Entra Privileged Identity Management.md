# Microsoft Entra Privileged Identity Management

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Lets admins switch on their admin rights only when needed and for a limited time, with approval and a reason.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Identity tools hold user accounts and decide who can sign in to what. When identity breaks, nobody can work.

**Made by:** [[Microsoft]] · **Runs:** Cloud (needs Entra ID P2 or Governance) · **Category:** [[Identity & Access]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MFA**: Multi-factor authentication: password plus a second proof, such as a phone prompt.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Just-in-time role activation for Entra ID and Azure roles, with approval, MFA on activation, time limits, access reviews and alerts.
