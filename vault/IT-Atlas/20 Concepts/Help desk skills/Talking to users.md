# Talking to users

> [!abstract] In one line
> Calm, clear and kind: people remember how you made them feel.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A good pilot announcement: calm, clear about what's happening, and when you'll hear more.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **phishing**: Fake messages designed to trick people into clicking or giving away passwords.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Let them explain first; don't interrupt.
- Use plain words, not jargon. 'Your laptop lost its connection to the office' rather than 'Your secure channel broke'.
- Say what you'll do next and when you'll update them.
- Never blame the user, even when it was a click on a phishing email; you want them to report quickly next time.
- Confirm it's fixed before closing.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- A user is angry because they can't work. What's the first thing to do?
