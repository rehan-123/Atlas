# Local vs cloud files

> [!abstract] In one line
> Local files live only on this device; cloud files live online and can sync to many devices.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Photos printed and kept in a drawer (local) versus photos in an online album you can open from any phone (cloud).

## How it works

- OneDrive is for your own files; SharePoint and Teams are for team files.
- Files On-Demand shows cloud files without downloading them until opened.
- Sync is not backup: deleting a synced file deletes it everywhere (it goes to the recycle bin).

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Is OneDrive sync the same as a backup?
> No. A delete or ransomware encryption syncs too. Version history and real backups protect you.
