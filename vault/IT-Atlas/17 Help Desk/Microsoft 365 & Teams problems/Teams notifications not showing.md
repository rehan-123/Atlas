# Teams notifications not showing

> [!abstract] What's going on
> A user isn't alerted about chats, calls or mentions in Teams.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A doorbell with the wires disconnected: the visitors come, nobody hears them.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Microsoft 365 & Teams problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **cache**: Saved copies of data kept to make things faster. Can go stale and cause odd problems.
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Teams > Settings > Notifications and activity: check the settings.
2. Windows: Settings > System > Notifications: allow Teams, and check Do not disturb is off.
3. Check your Teams status isn't set to Do not disturb.

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Check Focus/Do not disturb schedules in Windows.
- Clear the Teams cache if settings look right.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Do not disturb on | Turn it off or add priority contacts. |
| Windows blocking Teams notifications | Allow them. |

## Escalate when

- Calls not ringing for many users
