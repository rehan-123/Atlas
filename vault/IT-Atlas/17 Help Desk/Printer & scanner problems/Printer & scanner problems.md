# Printer & scanner problems

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Printers offline, stuck jobs, bad prints and scan to email.

Part of [[Help Desk]].

| Problem | What's going on |
|---|---|
| [[Printer offline]] | The printer shows 'Offline' or nothing happens when you print. |
| [[Print job stuck]] | A document sits in the print queue and blocks everything behind it. |
| [[Prints look wrong]] | Pages come out with strange symbols, faded, streaky or in the wrong size. |
| [[Scan to email not working]] | The copier scans but the email never arrives, or it shows an SMTP or login error. |
| [[Add a network printer]] | A user needs a printer added to their PC. |
