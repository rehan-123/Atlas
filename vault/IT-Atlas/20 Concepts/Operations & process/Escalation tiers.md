# Escalation tiers

> [!abstract] In one line
> How work moves between L1, L2 and L3 (and vendors).

> [!tip] Think of it like
> L1 is the front desk, L2 the specialist, L3 the consultant.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- **L1**: triage, known fixes from playbooks, gather evidence.
- **L2**: deeper troubleshooting and most changes.
- **L3**: complex root cause, architecture and vendor escalations.
- Escalate with evidence: what alerted, what was checked, what was tried, timestamps.
