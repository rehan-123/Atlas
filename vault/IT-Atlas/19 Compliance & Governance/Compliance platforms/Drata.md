# Drata

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A compliance automation platform, similar to Vanta, that collects audit evidence automatically.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** These platforms map your controls to frameworks like SOC 2 or ISO 27001 and collect the evidence automatically.
