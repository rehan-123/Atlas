# Yealink

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A common brand of desk phones and meeting room devices that work with Teams, Zoom and SIP phone systems.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Business phone systems that run over the network or the internet.

**Made by:** [[Yealink]] · **Category:** [[Voice & UC]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SIP**: The protocol that sets up internet phone calls.
> **IP**: Internet Protocol address: a device's address on the network, like 192.168.1.20.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

IP phones and video devices provisioned via auto-provisioning servers or Yealink Device Management.
