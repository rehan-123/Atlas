# Drivers

> [!abstract] In one line
> Small programs that teach the operating system how to talk to a piece of hardware.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A translator between Windows and a device. A bad translator and they misunderstand each other.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **Wi-Fi**: Wireless network connection using radio signals.
> **driver**: Small program that lets the operating system talk to a piece of hardware, like a printer or graphics card.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Printers, graphics cards, Wi-Fi, docks and sound all need drivers.
- Bad or outdated drivers cause blue screens, no sound, no Wi-Fi and display problems.
- Get drivers from Windows Update or the device maker, not random download sites.
- Device Manager shows devices with a yellow warning when a driver is missing or broken.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Wi-Fi stopped working right after a Windows update. What do you try in Device Manager?
> Roll back or update the Wi-Fi adapter driver.
