# Microsoft Defender for Office 365

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Microsoft's email protection: it scans links and attachments and keeps suspicious mail in quarantine.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Email security filters catch phishing, malware and fake senders before the mail reaches the user.

**Made by:** [[Microsoft]] · **Runs:** Cloud · **Category:** [[Security]] › [[Email security]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **quarantine**: A holding area for suspicious emails or files, so they can't do harm.
> **phishing**: Fake messages designed to trick people into clicking or giving away passwords.
> **malware**: Any harmful software: viruses, ransomware, spyware and the like.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Email protection for Exchange Online: Exchange Online Protection as the baseline, plus Safe Links, Safe Attachments, anti-phishing and quarantine in Defender for Office 365.

## Alerts you'll see

- Users reporting quarantined mail
- Phishing campaigns detected
- Compromised user alerts
