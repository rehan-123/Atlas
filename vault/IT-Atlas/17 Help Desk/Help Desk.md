# Help Desk

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Everyday IT problems and how to fix them, in plain English. Start here if you are new to IT: every guide begins with steps anyone can try, then what the tech checks.

Back to [[Home]].

1. [[Forgot password]]
2. [[Account locked out]]
3. [[MFA not working or new phone]]
4. [[Outlook won't open]]
5. [[No internet]]
6. [[Printer offline]]
7. [[PC is slow]]
8. [[Teams camera or mic not working]]

10. [[Clicked a phishing link]]

## By area

- [[Windows PC problems]] (17): Slow, frozen, crashing or broken Windows computers, screens, sound and keyboards.
- [[Internet & Wi-Fi problems]] (8): No internet, Wi-Fi trouble, websites, shared drives and VPN.
- [[Email & Outlook problems]] (10): Outlook not opening, mail not arriving or not sending, full mailboxes and shared mailboxes.
- [[Sign-in & password problems]] (7): Forgotten passwords, locked accounts, MFA on a new phone and sign-in errors.
- [[Printer & scanner problems]] (5): Printers offline, stuck jobs, bad prints and scan to email.
- [[Microsoft 365 & Teams problems]] (9): Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint files, Office activation and deleted files.
- [[Mac problems]] (4): Slow or stuck Macs, apps blocked from opening and full disks.
- [[Phone & tablet problems]] (3): Work email on phones, sync problems and lost devices.
- [[Security & scam problems]] (6): Phishing, fake virus pop-ups, hacked accounts and suspicious emails.
- [[Software & app problems]] (4): Installing software, apps that crash and misbehaving browsers.
- [[New starters & leavers]] (3): Setting people up on day one and closing their access when they leave.
