# WebTitan

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A DNS-based web filter popular with MSPs for blocking malicious and unwanted websites.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** DNS filtering blocks bad or unwanted websites by refusing to look up their addresses.

**Made by:** [[TitanHQ]] · **Category:** [[Security]] › [[DNS & web filtering]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MSP**: Managed service provider: a company that runs IT for other companies.
> **DNS**: The service that turns names (like server01.contoso.local) into IP addresses.
> **agent**: A small program installed on a computer that reports to, and takes orders from, a central console.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Cloud DNS filtering with policy per location or user, roaming agents and reporting.
