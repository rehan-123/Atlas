# Operations & process

How NOC work is run: incidents, changes, SLAs and handovers.

Part of [[Concepts]].

- [[ITIL incident, problem & change]]: The core ITIL processes most PSAs and ITSMs are built around.
- [[Severity levels]]: How urgent an incident is, usually P1 to P4.
- [[Escalation tiers]]: How work moves between L1, L2 and L3 (and vendors).
- [[SLA & SLO]]: Service level agreement (the contract) and service level objective (the internal target).
- [[Root cause analysis]]: Finding why an incident happened so it does not repeat.
- [[Shift handover]]: Passing open work between NOC shifts without losing context.
- [[Maintenance windows]]: Agreed times for disruptive changes.
