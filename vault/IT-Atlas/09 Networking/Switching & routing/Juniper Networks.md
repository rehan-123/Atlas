# Juniper Networks

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Routers, switches, firewalls and Mist Wi-Fi, now part of HPE.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Switches connect devices inside a building; routers connect different networks together.

**Made by:** [[HPE]] · **Runs:** Hardware and cloud (Mist) · **Category:** [[Networking]] › [[Switching & routing]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **Wi-Fi**: Wireless network connection using radio signals.
> **router**: Device that connects one network to another, such as the office to the internet.

## In more detail

Routers, switches and SRX firewalls running Junos, plus Mist AI-driven cloud-managed wireless and wired. HPE completed its acquisition of Juniper in 2025 (verify).
