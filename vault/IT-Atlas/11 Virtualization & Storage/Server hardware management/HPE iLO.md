# HPE iLO

> [!abstract] In plain words
> The management chip in HPE servers: hardware health logs and remote console.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Out-of-band controllers are tiny computers inside a server that let you see its health and console even when Windows is down.

**Made by:** [[HPE]] · **Runs:** Out-of-band controller · **Category:** [[Virtualization & Storage]] › [[Server hardware management]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **PSU**: Power supply unit.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

HPE's Integrated Lights-Out: remote console, Integrated Management Log (IML), Active Health System and Smart Array status.

## Alerts you'll see

- IML hardware events
- Smart Array or drive faults
- PSU or fan failures
