# PagerDuty

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Sends alerts to whoever is on call, by app, SMS or phone call, and escalates if nobody answers.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** On-call tools decide who gets woken up for an alert, and keep paging until someone responds.
