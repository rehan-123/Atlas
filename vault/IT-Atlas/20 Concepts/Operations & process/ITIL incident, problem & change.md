# ITIL incident, problem & change

> [!abstract] In one line
> The core ITIL processes most PSAs and ITSMs are built around.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Incident: put out the fire. Problem: find out why fires keep starting. Change: rewire safely so it doesn't happen again.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **PSA**: Professional services automation: the MSP's ticketing, time and billing system.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- **Incident**: restore service fast.
- **Problem**: find and remove the root cause of recurring incidents.
- **Change**: control risk when modifying systems (standard, normal and emergency changes).
- Link repeated incidents to one problem record so the pattern is visible.

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- The same server runs out of disk every month. Incident or problem?
> Each outage is an incident; the repeating pattern is a problem to fix at the root.
