# Carbonite

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Simple cloud backup for small businesses, now owned by OpenText.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Endpoint backup protects laptops and desktops, usually by sending copies straight to the cloud.

**Made by:** [[OpenText]] · **Runs:** SaaS · **Category:** [[Backup & DR]] › [[Endpoint backup]]
