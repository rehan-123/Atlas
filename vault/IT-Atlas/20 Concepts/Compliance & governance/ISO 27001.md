# ISO 27001

> [!abstract] In one line
> An international standard for running an information security management system (ISMS), certified by an external auditor.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A quality management system, but for security: the process for finding and fixing risks matters as much as the controls.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **ISMS**: Information security management system: the processes ISO 27001 certifies.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Built around risk assessment, a Statement of Applicability and continual improvement.
- Annex A lists controls (93 in the 2022 version) grouped as organizational, people, physical and technological.
- Certification lasts three years with yearly surveillance audits.
