# PC won't turn on

> [!abstract] What's going on
> Pressing the power button does nothing, or lights come on but the screen stays black.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A car that won't start: first check there's fuel (power) and the battery isn't flat before you open the engine.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Windows PC problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Check the power cable is plugged in at both ends and the wall socket is switched on.
2. Laptop: plug in the charger, wait 10 minutes, then hold the power button for 15 seconds and try again.
3. Check the monitor is on and set to the right input (desktop PCs).
4. Unplug docks, USB drives and extra cables, then try again.

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Look for any lights, fan noise or beeps; they tell you whether the PC has power at all.
- Try a known-good charger or power cable.
- Desktop: connect the monitor directly to the PC (not through a dock) and try another cable.
- Laptop: try with the lid open and an external monitor to rule out a broken screen.
- Check warranty status with the manufacturer's serial number lookup before opening the case.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Flat battery or faulty charger | Charge with a known-good charger. |
| Monitor off or wrong input | Power the monitor and pick the right input. |
| Dock or USB device stopping the boot | Unplug everything and retry. |
| Hardware failure (motherboard, power supply) | Log a warranty repair or replace the device. |

## Escalate when

- No lights or sound at all with a known-good charger
- The PC has a burning smell or liquid damage
- The user has urgent work: give them a loaner device
