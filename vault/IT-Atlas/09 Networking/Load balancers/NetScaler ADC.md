# NetScaler ADC

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A load balancer and remote access gateway, often in front of Citrix (formerly Citrix ADC).
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A load balancer spreads incoming traffic across several servers, so one busy or failed server doesn't take a service down.

**Made by:** [[Cloud Software Group]] · **Runs:** Appliance or VM · **Category:** [[Networking]] › [[Load balancers]]

## In more detail

Application delivery controller and gateway (formerly Citrix ADC), often the front door for Citrix virtual apps.

## Good to know

- October 2023: Citrix Bleed (CVE-2023-4966) leaked session tokens and was mass-exploited. Patching alone was not enough; sessions had to be killed.
