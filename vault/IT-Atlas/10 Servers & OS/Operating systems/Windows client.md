# Windows client

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Windows 10 and 11 on users' PCs. Windows 10 support ended in October 2025.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** The operating systems you'll support: Windows Server, Windows 10 and 11, Linux and macOS.

**Made by:** [[Microsoft]] · **Runs:** Endpoints · **Category:** [[Servers & Operating Systems]] › [[Operating systems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **endpoint**: Any device a user works on: laptop, desktop, phone or server.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Windows 10 and 11 endpoints. Windows 10 reached end of support on 14 October 2025; machines still on it need ESU or an upgrade.
