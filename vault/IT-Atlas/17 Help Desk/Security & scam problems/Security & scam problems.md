# Security & scam problems

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Phishing, fake virus pop-ups, hacked accounts and suspicious emails.

Part of [[Help Desk]].

| Problem | What's going on |
|---|---|
| [[Clicked a phishing link]] | Someone clicked a link or opened an attachment in a suspicious email, or typed their password into a fake page. |
| [[Suspicious email]] | An email feels off: urgent, unexpected, asks for money, passwords or to open a file. |
| [[Fake virus pop-up]] | A browser page shouts that your PC is infected and tells you to call a number. |
| [[Account hacked or sending spam]] | People receive strange emails from the user, or there are sign-ins the user didn't make. |
| [[Suspicious USB drive]] | Someone found an unknown USB stick or received one unexpectedly. |
| [[Text message or phone call scam]] | A user got a text or call pretending to be IT, a bank or a boss, asking for codes, passwords or payments. |
