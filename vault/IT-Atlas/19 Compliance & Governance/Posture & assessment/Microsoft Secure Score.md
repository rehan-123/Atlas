# Microsoft Secure Score

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A score in the Microsoft Defender portal that shows how well your Microsoft 365 tenant is secured, with a ranked list of fixes.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Posture tools score your configuration against best practice and tell you what to fix first.

**Made by:** [[Microsoft]] · **Category:** [[Compliance & Governance]] › [[Posture & assessment]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **tenant**: One organization's own space in a cloud service such as Microsoft 365.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Security posture measurement for identity, devices, apps and data in Microsoft 365, with improvement actions and their score impact.

## Good to know

- Use it as a to-do list, not a target: some actions don't fit every client, and you can mark them as accepted risk.
