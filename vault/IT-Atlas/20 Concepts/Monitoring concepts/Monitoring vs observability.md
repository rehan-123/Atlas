# Monitoring vs observability

> [!abstract] In one line
> Monitoring tells you something is wrong; observability helps you find out why.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Monitoring is the warning light on the dashboard; observability is the mechanic's diagnostic tools.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **threshold**: The value that triggers an alert.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Monitoring: known checks and thresholds.
- Observability: metrics, logs and traces rich enough to ask new questions about unknown problems.
