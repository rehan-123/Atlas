# Keyboard or mouse not working

> [!abstract] What's going on
> Keys or the mouse pointer don't respond, or respond strangely.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A remote control with a flat battery: the TV is fine, the remote just can't talk to it.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Windows PC problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **port**: A numbered door on a device that a specific service listens on, for example 443 for HTTPS or 3389 for RDP.
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Wireless: replace or charge the batteries and switch it off and on.
2. Wired: plug it into a different USB port, directly on the PC rather than the dock.
3. Check Num Lock and Caps Lock, and whether the keyboard has a function lock key.

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Device Manager: look for warning icons under Keyboards and Mice; uninstall and replug.
- Bluetooth: remove and re-pair the device.
- Check the keyboard language (Windows+Space switches layouts) if keys type the wrong characters.
- Use the on-screen keyboard to log in if the physical one is dead.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Flat battery or receiver unplugged | Replace batteries; replug the receiver. |
| Wrong keyboard layout | Windows+Space or Settings > Time & language. |
| Dock USB problem | Plug directly into the PC. |
| Broken device | Replace it. |

## Escalate when

- Built-in laptop keyboard is dead (hardware repair)
- Liquid was spilled on the device

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- A user's @ and " keys are swapped. What's the likely cause?
> The keyboard layout is set to UK instead of US (or the other way round). Press Windows+Space or change it in language settings.
