# Sign-in & password problems

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Forgotten passwords, locked accounts, MFA on a new phone and sign-in errors.

Part of [[Help Desk]].

| Problem | What's going on |
|---|---|
| [[Forgot password]] | The user can't remember their password. |
| [[Account locked out]] | Too many wrong passwords and the account is temporarily blocked. |
| [[MFA not working or new phone]] | The sign-in approval doesn't arrive, or the user got a new phone and can't approve sign-ins. |
| [[Can't sign in to Microsoft 365]] | Errors when signing in to Outlook, Teams or office.com, or sign-in loops. |
| [[Trust relationship failed]] | At login Windows says 'The trust relationship between this workstation and the primary domain failed'. |
| [[Change your password]] | A user's password is about to expire or they want to change it. |
| [[MFA when travelling or no signal]] | A user abroad or without mobile signal can't approve sign-ins. |
