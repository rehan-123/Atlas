# Backups for everyone

> [!abstract] In one line
> Copies of data kept somewhere else so it can be restored after loss, theft or ransomware.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A spare house key kept at a trusted friend's place, not under your own doormat.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **ransomware**: Malware that encrypts files and demands payment to unlock them.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Sync (OneDrive) is not the same as backup.
- Business backups copy servers, PCs and Microsoft 365 automatically and keep older versions.
- A backup only counts if it can be restored; test restores prove it.
- Users should save work to OneDrive or company shares, not only to the Desktop.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Microsoft keeps your files in the cloud. Do you still need a backup?
> Yes. Microsoft keeps the service running, but you are responsible for recovering data you delete, lose or have encrypted.
