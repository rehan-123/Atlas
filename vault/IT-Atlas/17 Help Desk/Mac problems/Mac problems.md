# Mac problems

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Slow or stuck Macs, apps blocked from opening and full disks.

Part of [[Help Desk]].

| Problem | What's going on |
|---|---|
| [[Mac is slow]] | A Mac is laggy, the fan is loud or you see the spinning beach ball. |
| [[Mac app won't open]] | macOS says an app 'can't be opened' or is from an unidentified developer. |
| [[Mac disk full]] | The Mac warns that the startup disk is almost full. |
| [[Mac won't start]] | The Mac shows a black screen, a circle with a line through it, or a folder with a question mark. |
