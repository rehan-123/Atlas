# Writing a good ticket

> [!abstract] In one line
> A ticket someone else can pick up without calling the user again.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A handover note for the next shift nurse: enough detail that they can carry on without waking you.

## How it works

- **Who and what**: user, device name, contact.
- **Problem in the user's words**, plus the exact error message.
- **What you checked** and what you found (including what was fine).
- **What you did**, with times.
- **Result**: fixed and confirmed with the user, or what's next and who owns it.
- Avoid notes like 'fixed' or 'done'; future you won't remember what that meant.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- What's missing from the note 'Outlook issue, fixed'?
> What the problem was, what was checked, what fixed it and that the user confirmed it.
