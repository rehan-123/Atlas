# Remote support etiquette

> [!abstract] In one line
> How to behave when you're on someone else's screen.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A tradesperson in someone's home: knock, explain, touch only what you came to fix, tidy up and leave.

## How it works

- Always ask before connecting, and tell them when you take control.
- Ask them to close personal or confidential windows first.
- Explain what you're doing as you go.
- Never read or open personal files that aren't related to the problem.
- Disconnect when finished and say so.
- Only use the company's approved remote tool, never one a user found online.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Why should you only use the company's approved remote tool?
> Unknown remote tools are a common way scammers and attackers get in, and they aren't logged or controlled.
