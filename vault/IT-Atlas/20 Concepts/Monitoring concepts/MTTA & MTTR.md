# MTTA & MTTR

> [!abstract] In one line
> Mean time to acknowledge and mean time to resolve (or restore).

> [!tip] Think of it like
> MTTA: how fast someone picks up the phone. MTTR: how long until the problem is fixed.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **NOC**: Network operations center: the team that monitors and fixes infrastructure.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- MTTA measures how fast the NOC notices and owns an alert.
- MTTR measures how long the impact lasts.
- Both are averages; also track the worst cases, since clients remember those.
