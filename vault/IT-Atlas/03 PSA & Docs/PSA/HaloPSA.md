# HaloPSA

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A very customizable ticketing and billing system for MSPs.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A PSA is the MSP's ticketing and billing system: every request, alert and hour worked ends up here.

**Made by:** [[Halo]] · **Runs:** SaaS or on-prem · **Category:** [[PSA & Documentation]] › [[PSA]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MSP**: Managed service provider: a company that runs IT for other companies.
> **PSA**: Professional services automation: the MSP's ticketing, time and billing system.
> **SaaS**: Software as a service: apps you use online, such as Microsoft 365.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Highly configurable PSA with ticketing, contracts, billing and project modules. A common migration target from other PSAs.
