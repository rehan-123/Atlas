# Maintenance windows

> [!abstract] In one line
> Agreed times for disruptive changes.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Agreed quiet hours for doing risky work.

## How it works

- Suppress expected alerts during the window, but watch for anything unrelated.
- Confirm systems are back and alerts are cleared before closing the window.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- What do you check before closing a maintenance window?
> That systems are back up and all alerts have cleared.
