# Troubleshooting method

> [!abstract] In one line
> Six steps that work for any IT problem: understand, reproduce, isolate, fix, verify, record.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A doctor: ask about symptoms, examine, test one thing at a time, treat, check the patient is better, write it in the notes.

## How it works

1. **Understand**: what exactly happens, since when, for whom, and what changed?
2. **Reproduce**: see it yourself (screen share or remote session).
3. **Isolate**: change one thing at a time. Does it happen on another PC, another user, another network, the web version?
4. **Fix**: start with the least risky fix.
5. **Verify**: confirm with the user that it's really fixed.
6. **Record**: write what you found and did in the ticket, so the next person benefits.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Outlook fails on one PC but works on the web. Which step have you just done?
> Isolate: you proved the mailbox is fine and the problem is on that PC.

> [!question]- Why is 'what changed recently?' such a powerful question?
> Most problems start right after a change: an update, a new device, a password change or a move.
