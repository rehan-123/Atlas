# Microsoft Copilot Studio

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A tool for building your own AI agents and chatbots that can answer from your documents and run actions.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** AI assistants answer questions, write drafts and summarize information from your work data. Admins control what data they can see.

**Made by:** [[Microsoft]] · **Category:** [[Cloud & SaaS]] › [[AI assistants]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SaaS**: Software as a service: apps you use online, such as Microsoft 365.
> **DLP**: Data loss prevention: rules that detect and stop sensitive data leaving.
> **agent**: A small program installed on a computer that reports to, and takes orders from, a central console.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Low-code agent builder on the Power Platform: knowledge sources, topics, actions via connectors and Power Automate; governed by Power Platform environments and DLP.
