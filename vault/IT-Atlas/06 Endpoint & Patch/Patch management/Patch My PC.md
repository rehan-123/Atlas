# Patch My PC

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Keeps third-party apps like Chrome and Zoom updated through Intune or SCCM.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Patching means installing updates, mostly security fixes. These tools decide what gets installed, when, and report what's missing.

**Made by:** [[Patch My PC]] · **Runs:** Publisher service or cloud · **Category:** [[Endpoint & Patch Management]] › [[Patch management]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **endpoint**: Any device a user works on: laptop, desktop, phone or server.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Third-party application patching published into Intune and Configuration Manager, with a large catalog of pre-packaged apps.
