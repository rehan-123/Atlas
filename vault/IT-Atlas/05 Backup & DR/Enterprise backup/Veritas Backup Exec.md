# Veritas Backup Exec

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Long-standing Windows backup software, often used with tape drives.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Enterprise backup platforms protect very large environments with many kinds of systems. You'll meet them at bigger clients.

**Made by:** [[Arctera]] · **Runs:** On-prem · **Category:** [[Backup & DR]] › [[Enterprise backup]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **DR**: Disaster recovery: getting systems back after a major failure.
> **SMB**: The Windows file-sharing protocol (port 445).

Long-running Windows backup software for SMBs, tape and disk. Moved to Arctera when Veritas split in 2024 (verify).
