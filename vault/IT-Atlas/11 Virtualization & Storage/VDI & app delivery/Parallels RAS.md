# Parallels RAS

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Software for publishing Windows apps and desktops to users anywhere, often a simpler alternative to Citrix.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** VDI gives users a desktop that actually runs in the data center or cloud, which they reach over the network.

**Made by:** [[Parallels]] · **Category:** [[Virtualization & Storage]] › [[VDI & app delivery]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **VDI**: Virtual desktop infrastructure: desktops that run on servers and are used remotely.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Remote application and desktop delivery over RDSH or VDI with a gateway, load balancing and client apps.
