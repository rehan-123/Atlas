## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Someone emails asking you to reset their manager's password. What do you do?
> Verify identity through the approved process; don't reset on an email request.

> [!question]- A user keeps getting locked out after changing their password. Likely cause?
> An old password saved on their phone or another device.

> [!question]- Outlook won't open but webmail works. Is mail lost?
> No. It's the PC's profile, cache or add-ins.
