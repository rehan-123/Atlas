# Zerto

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Copies virtual machines to another site every few seconds, so very little data is lost in a disaster.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** These are the backup and disaster-recovery services built into Azure and AWS, plus tools that keep a live copy of servers in another location.

**Made by:** [[HPE]] · **Runs:** On-prem and cloud · **Category:** [[Backup & DR]] › [[Cloud backup & DR]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **DR**: Disaster recovery: getting systems back after a major failure.
> **RPO**: Recovery point objective: the most data, measured in time, you can afford to lose.
> **replication**: Copying data or changes to another place, continuously or on a schedule.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Journal-based continuous data protection and replication for VMware, Hyper-V and clouds, with RPOs in seconds. Owned by HPE since 2021.
