# Excel or Word file won't open or is corrupted

> [!abstract] What's going on
> A document won't open, shows an error or opens with missing content.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A book with a damaged spine: there's often a way to rebuild it from earlier copies.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Microsoft 365 & Teams problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **browser**: The app you use to open websites: Edge, Chrome, Safari or Firefox.
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> **ransomware**: Malware that encrypts files and demands payment to unlock them.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Try opening it in the browser version (Office for the web).
2. Use File > Open > select file > Open and Repair (desktop app).
3. Check for an earlier version (Version history or Previous Versions).

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Check the file isn't still syncing or locked by another user.
- Copy it locally and try again to rule out the network.
- Restore from backup if repair fails.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Sync conflict | Use the right copy or version. |
| Corruption | Open and Repair, or restore a version. |

## Escalate when

- Many files corrupted at once (possible ransomware)
