# Adobe Acrobat asks to sign in or license

> [!abstract] What's going on
> Acrobat keeps asking the user to sign in, or says it isn't licensed.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A membership card that's been moved to a new name: the app needs to see the right account.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Software & app problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Sign in with your work email (choose Company or School Account if asked).
2. Sign out and back in: Help > Sign Out.

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Check the user is assigned a licence in the Adobe Admin Console.
- Users who only need to read PDFs can use the free Reader mode.
- Clear cached credentials if it keeps prompting.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| No licence assigned | Assign one in Adobe Admin Console. |
| Personal account used | Sign in with the work account. |

## Escalate when

- Licence count exceeded

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Does a user need a paid Acrobat licence just to read PDFs?
> No. Reading PDFs is free; editing and some features need a licence.
