## Practice

- [ ] Open Task Manager and find the app using the most memory.
- [ ] Run `ipconfig /all` and find your IP address, gateway and DNS servers.
- [ ] Find out whether your Desktop is synced to OneDrive.
- [ ] Run `dsregcmd /status` and work out how your PC is joined (domain, Entra or both).

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- Which part of the computer is cleared when you restart?
> RAM (memory).

> [!question]- What does 169.254.x.x mean?
> The PC didn't get an address from DHCP.

> [!question]- Is OneDrive sync a backup?
> No. Deletes and encryption sync too.
