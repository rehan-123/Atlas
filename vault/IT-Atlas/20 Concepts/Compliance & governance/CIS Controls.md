# CIS Controls

> [!abstract] In one line
> A prioritized list of 18 security controls from the Center for Internet Security, grouped into Implementation Groups by maturity.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A first-aid course: the handful of skills that prevent most harm, before anything advanced.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MSP**: Managed service provider: a company that runs IT for other companies.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- IG1 is 'essential cyber hygiene' for small organizations: inventory, secure configuration, access control, patching, backups, awareness.
- Practical and specific, which makes it a good MSP baseline for small and mid-sized clients.
- The related CIS Benchmarks give exact settings per product.
