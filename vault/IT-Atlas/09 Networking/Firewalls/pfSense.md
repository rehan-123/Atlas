# pfSense

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A free, open-source firewall you install on your own hardware.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A firewall is the security guard at the edge of the network, deciding what traffic is allowed in and out. It often also handles the VPN and internet links.

**Made by:** [[Netgate]] · **Runs:** Appliance or self-installed · **Category:** [[Networking]] › [[Firewalls]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **VPN**: An encrypted tunnel over the internet to a private network.
> **appliance**: A dedicated box (or virtual machine) built to do one job, such as backup or firewall.
> **router**: Device that connects one network to another, such as the office to the internet.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

FreeBSD-based open-source firewall and router from Netgate, with packages for VPN, IDS and more.
