# Google Workspace

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Google's office suite: Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Meet.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Office suites for mail, documents and calendars other than Microsoft 365.

**Made by:** [[Google]] · **Runs:** SaaS · **Category:** [[Cloud & SaaS]] › [[Productivity suites]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SaaS**: Software as a service: apps you use online, such as Microsoft 365.
> **CLI**: Command-line interface: typing commands instead of clicking.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Gmail, Drive, Calendar and Meet, administered in the Google Admin console; GAM is the common admin CLI.
