# Rubrik

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Enterprise backup focused on recovering quickly from ransomware.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Enterprise backup platforms protect very large environments with many kinds of systems. You'll meet them at bigger clients.

**Made by:** [[Rubrik]] · **Runs:** Appliance or cloud · **Category:** [[Backup & DR]] › [[Enterprise backup]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **DR**: Disaster recovery: getting systems back after a major failure.
> **SaaS**: Software as a service: apps you use online, such as Microsoft 365.
> **ransomware**: Malware that encrypts files and demands payment to unlock them.
> **appliance**: A dedicated box (or virtual machine) built to do one job, such as backup or firewall.
> **immutable**: Can't be changed or deleted until a set date, even by an admin.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Enterprise backup built around immutable storage and ransomware recovery, with SaaS and Microsoft 365 protection.
