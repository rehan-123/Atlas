# Ninite Pro

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A simple service that silently installs and updates common free apps like browsers, Zoom and 7-Zip.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Software deployment tools install or update apps on many computers at once, without clicking through installers.

**Made by:** [[Ninite]] · **Category:** [[Endpoint & Patch Management]] › [[Software deployment]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **RMM**: Remote monitoring and management: the MSP's main tool for watching and fixing client machines.
> **browser**: The app you use to open websites: Edge, Chrome, Safari or Firefox.
> **endpoint**: Any device a user works on: laptop, desktop, phone or server.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Silent installer/updater for popular third-party apps, scriptable through RMMs.
