# Jira Service Management

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Atlassian's ticketing system, built on Jira.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** ITSM tools are the ticketing systems big internal IT departments use, built around formal processes for incidents and changes.

**Made by:** [[Atlassian]] · **Runs:** SaaS or Data Center · **Category:** [[PSA & Documentation]] › [[ITSM]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SaaS**: Software as a service: apps you use online, such as Microsoft 365.
> **PSA**: Professional services automation: the MSP's ticketing, time and billing system.
> **SLA**: Service level agreement: the response and fix times promised to a client. (On firewalls, an SD-WAN 'SLA' is a link-quality check.)
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Atlassian's service management on Jira: request types, queues, SLAs, change management and now on-call alerting.
