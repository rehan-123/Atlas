# Email & Outlook problems

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Outlook not opening, mail not arriving or not sending, full mailboxes and shared mailboxes.

Part of [[Help Desk]].

| Problem | What's going on |
|---|---|
| [[Outlook won't open]] | Outlook gets stuck on 'Loading profile', crashes, or keeps asking for a password. |
| [[Outlook not receiving email]] | New emails aren't arriving, or someone says they sent something that never came. |
| [[Email stuck in Outbox]] | An email sits in the Outbox and won't send. |
| [[Mailbox full]] | Warnings that the mailbox is almost full, or email bouncing because it's full. |
| [[Shared mailbox missing]] | A shared mailbox (like info@ or accounts@) doesn't show in Outlook, or you can't send as it. |
| [[Email went to junk]] | A real email landed in Junk, or your emails go to other people's spam. |
| [[Outlook search not working]] | Searching in Outlook finds nothing, or misses recent emails. |
| [[Recover a deleted email]] | Someone deleted an email or folder and needs it back. |
| [[Set up out of office]] | A user wants automatic replies while away. |
| [[Share a calendar or give delegate access]] | A user wants a colleague or assistant to see or manage their calendar. |
