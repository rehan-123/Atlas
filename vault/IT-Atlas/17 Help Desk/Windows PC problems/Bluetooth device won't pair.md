# Bluetooth device won't pair

> [!abstract] What's going on
> A Bluetooth headset, mouse or keyboard won't connect.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Two people trying to shake hands while one has turned away: both need to be ready at the same time.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Windows PC problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **driver**: Small program that lets the operating system talk to a piece of hardware, like a printer or graphics card.
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Turn Bluetooth off and on (Windows+A).
2. Put the device in pairing mode (usually hold its button until a light flashes).
3. Remove the device in Settings > Bluetooth & devices and pair again.

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Check Device Manager for the Bluetooth adapter and update its driver.
- Check the device isn't still connected to a phone.
- Test with another device to isolate the adapter.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Device paired to another phone | Disconnect it there. |
| Adapter driver problem | Update or reinstall it. |
| Low battery | Charge the device. |

## Escalate when

- Bluetooth adapter missing from Device Manager (hardware)
