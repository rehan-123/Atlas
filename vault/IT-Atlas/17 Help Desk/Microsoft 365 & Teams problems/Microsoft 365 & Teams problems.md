# Microsoft 365 & Teams problems

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Teams, OneDrive, SharePoint files, Office activation and deleted files.

Part of [[Help Desk]].

| Problem | What's going on |
|---|---|
| [[Teams camera or mic not working]] | Your camera is black or others can't hear you in Teams. |
| [[Teams won't sign in or keeps loading]] | Teams is stuck on a loading screen, shows an error, or is very slow. |
| [[OneDrive not syncing]] | The OneDrive icon shows a red X, a pause sign, or files with sync errors. |
| [[File locked or in use]] | A file says it's locked for editing by someone else, or 'in use by another program'. |
| [[Recover a deleted file]] | Someone deleted or overwrote a file and needs it back. |
| [[Office activation error]] | Word or Excel says 'Unlicensed product', 'Account error' or keeps asking you to activate. |
| [[Access denied to a shared file]] | 'You need permission to access this item' when opening a shared link. |
| [[Excel or Word file won't open or is corrupted]] | A document won't open, shows an error or opens with missing content. |
| [[Teams notifications not showing]] | A user isn't alerted about chats, calls or mentions in Teams. |
