# QNAP NAS

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A network storage box for small businesses.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Storage arrays and NAS boxes are dedicated machines full of disks that store data for servers and users.

**Made by:** [[QNAP]] · **Runs:** Appliance · **Category:** [[Virtualization & Storage]] › [[Storage arrays & NAS]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **NAS**: Network-attached storage: a box full of disks that shares files over the network.
> **SMB**: The Windows file-sharing protocol (port 445).
> **appliance**: A dedicated box (or virtual machine) built to do one job, such as backup or firewall.
> **ransomware**: Malware that encrypts files and demands payment to unlock them.
> **firmware**: The software built into hardware such as firewalls, disks and servers.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

SMB NAS running QTS or QuTS hero.

## Good to know

- 2021 and 2022: Qlocker and DeadBolt ransomware hit internet-exposed QNAP devices. Keep firmware current and do not expose them.
