# Start here

Welcome to the IT Atlas: everyday troubleshooting, IT fundamentals, and operational playbooks explained in plain English.

**Choose the route that matches your task.** Each guide links to related tools, concepts, and next steps so you can move from a symptom to a sound action.

1. **Learning IT from the beginning?** Open the [[Learning path]] and start with Week 1.
2. **Helping someone with a computer problem?** Open [[Help Desk]] and choose the matching symptom.
3. **Working an active alert?** Open [[Playbooks]] and choose the closest playbook.
4. **Looking up a product?** Press `Ctrl+O` in Obsidian and type its name.
5. **Unsure what a term means?** Check the [[Glossary]] or the “Words on this page” box at the top of a guide.

For the full map, open [[Home]]. For shortcuts, page types, and experience levels, read [[How to use the IT Atlas]].

## One-time setup

- Turn on the **Dataview** community plugin if the installer did not enable it (Settings → Community plugins). It powers live tables on [[Home]] and category pages.
- To have Claude add or update notes, open a terminal in the vault folder and run `claude`. Try `/add-tool`, `/add-playbook`, `/verify`, `/gaps`, or `/lint`.

## About accuracy

The content reflects general knowledge as of 2026-06. Product names, owners, ports, paths, and lifecycle details can change. Items marked “(verify)” are known to be uncertain. Each tool has a `verified` field; `/verify` checks a note against vendor documentation and records the date and source.

Before using a command or procedure on a live system, verify its scope and follow your organization's change and approval process.
