# Slack

> [!abstract] In plain words
> A chat app for teams, organized into channels.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Office suites for mail, documents and calendars other than Microsoft 365.

**Made by:** [[Salesforce]] · **Category:** [[Cloud & SaaS]] › [[Productivity suites]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SaaS**: Software as a service: apps you use online, such as Microsoft 365.
> **SSO**: Single sign-on: one login for many apps.
> **SCIM**: A standard for automatically creating and removing user accounts in apps.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Team messaging with channels, huddles and app integrations; Enterprise Grid for large organizations; SSO and SCIM provisioning.
