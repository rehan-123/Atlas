# ConnectWise PSA

> [!abstract] In plain words
> ConnectWise's ticketing and billing system for MSPs (used to be called ConnectWise Manage). Tickets, time sheets, contracts and SLAs live here.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A PSA is the MSP's ticketing and billing system: every request, alert and hour worked ends up here.

**Made by:** [[ConnectWise]] · **Runs:** Cloud or on-prem · **Category:** [[PSA & Documentation]] › [[PSA]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **PSA**: Professional services automation: the MSP's ticketing, time and billing system.
> **MSP**: Managed service provider: a company that runs IT for other companies.
> **SLA**: Service level agreement: the response and fix times promised to a client. (On firewalls, an SD-WAN 'SLA' is a link-quality check.)
> **API**: A way for programs and scripts to talk to a service directly, without the web page.
> **RMM**: Remote monitoring and management: the MSP's main tool for watching and fixing client machines.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

The MSP PSA formerly called ConnectWise Manage: service boards, tickets, agreements, time entries, workflow rules, SLAs, procurement and billing.

## Main parts

- Service boards, statuses and ticket types
- Agreements and additions
- Time entries and billing
- Workflow rules and SLAs
- REST API

## Alerts you'll see

- SLA breach warnings
- Tickets auto-created by RMM and backup integrations
