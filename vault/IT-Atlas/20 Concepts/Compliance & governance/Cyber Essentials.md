# Cyber Essentials

> [!abstract] In one line
> A UK government-backed certification covering five basic controls; often required for UK public-sector contracts.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A driving test for basic road safety: not everything, but the things that prevent most crashes.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MFA**: Multi-factor authentication: password plus a second proof, such as a phone prompt.
> **malware**: Any harmful software: viruses, ransomware, spyware and the like.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Five areas: firewalls, secure configuration, user access control, malware protection and security update management.
- Cyber Essentials Plus adds a hands-on technical audit.
- Requires critical and high-risk patches within 14 days, and MFA on cloud services.
