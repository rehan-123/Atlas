# NIST Cybersecurity Framework

> [!abstract] In one line
> A widely used US framework that organizes security into six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A health check-up structured by body system, so nothing gets missed.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MSP**: Managed service provider: a company that runs IT for other companies.
> **MFA**: Multi-factor authentication: password plus a second proof, such as a phone prompt.
> **EDR**: Endpoint detection and response: modern antivirus that records activity and can stop and undo attacks.
> **SIEM**: A system that collects security logs from everywhere and looks for attacks.
> **DR**: Disaster recovery: getting systems back after a major failure.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Version 2.0 (2024) added **Govern** to the original five functions.
- Not a certification: a common language for assessing maturity and planning improvements.
- Maps well to MSP services: asset inventory (Identify), MFA and patching (Protect), EDR and SIEM (Detect), incident response (Respond), backup and DR (Recover).
