# Ping Identity

> [!abstract] In plain words
> An enterprise single sign-on and identity platform, often found in large companies alongside or instead of Okta.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Identity tools hold user accounts and decide who can sign in to what. When identity breaks, nobody can work.

**Made by:** [[Ping Identity]] · **Category:** [[Identity & Access]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SSO**: Single sign-on: one login for many apps.
> **MFA**: Multi-factor authentication: password plus a second proof, such as a phone prompt.
> **ping**: A quick test that sends a small message to a device and waits for a reply.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

Enterprise identity and access management: SSO, MFA, federation (PingFederate) and directory.
