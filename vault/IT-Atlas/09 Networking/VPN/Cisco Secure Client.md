# Cisco Secure Client

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Cisco's VPN app (formerly AnyConnect).
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A VPN creates an encrypted tunnel over the internet, so a remote user or branch office can reach the office network safely.

**Made by:** [[Cisco]] · **Runs:** Client software · **Category:** [[Networking]] › [[VPN]]

Cisco's endpoint client, formerly AnyConnect: VPN to ASA and FTD, plus modules for Umbrella roaming and posture.
