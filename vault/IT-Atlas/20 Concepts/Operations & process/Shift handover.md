# Shift handover

> [!abstract] In one line
> Passing open work between NOC shifts without losing context.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A relay race: the baton is the open work, and dropping it is the most common failure.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **NOC**: Network operations center: the team that monitors and fixes infrastructure.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- For each open item: status, next check time, who owns it, and what not to touch.
- Hand over active P1 and P2 items verbally as well as in writing.
