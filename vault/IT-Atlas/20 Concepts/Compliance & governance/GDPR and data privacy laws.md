# GDPR and data privacy laws

> [!abstract] In one line
> Laws that control how personal data is collected and handled, such as the EU GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Someone lent you their diary: you may only use it for what they agreed, keep it safe, and give it back or destroy it when asked.

## How it works

- GDPR applies to personal data of people in the EU, wherever the processor is.
- Key ideas: lawful basis, data minimization, rights to access and erasure, breach notification (72 hours to the regulator under GDPR), data processing agreements with providers.
- India's DPDP Act, 2023 sets similar duties for digital personal data in India.
- For IT: know where personal data is, limit access, encrypt it, log access and be able to delete it.
