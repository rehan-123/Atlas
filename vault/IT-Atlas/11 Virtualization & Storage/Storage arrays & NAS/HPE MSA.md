# HPE MSA

> [!abstract] In plain words
> HPE's entry-level shared storage for small server clusters.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Storage arrays and NAS boxes are dedicated machines full of disks that store data for servers and users.

**Made by:** [[HPE]] · **Runs:** Appliance · **Category:** [[Virtualization & Storage]] › [[Storage arrays & NAS]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **NAS**: Network-attached storage: a box full of disks that shares files over the network.
> **SAN**: Storage area network: shared block storage for servers.
> **cluster**: Several servers working together so one can take over if another fails.
> **appliance**: A dedicated box (or virtual machine) built to do one job, such as backup or firewall.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

HPE's entry-level SAN arrays, common under small Hyper-V and VMware clusters.
