# Webcam not detected

> [!abstract] What's going on
> The camera shows nothing, or apps say no camera was found.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> A window with the curtain drawn: the camera may be fine, just covered or switched off.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Windows PC problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **BIOS**: The low-level setup program that starts a PC before Windows loads (UEFI on modern PCs).
> **driver**: Small program that lets the operating system talk to a piece of hardware, like a printer or graphics card.
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Check for a privacy shutter or camera key on the laptop.
2. Close other apps that might be using the camera.
3. Restart the PC.

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Settings > Privacy & security > Camera: allow access.
- Device Manager > Cameras: update, or uninstall and scan for hardware changes.
- Check the BIOS hasn't disabled the camera.
- Test in the Windows Camera app.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Privacy shutter or key | Open it. |
| Privacy setting off | Allow camera access. |
| Driver problem | Reinstall the driver. |

## Escalate when

- Camera missing from Device Manager and BIOS (hardware)
