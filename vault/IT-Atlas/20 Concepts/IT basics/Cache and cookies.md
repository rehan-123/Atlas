# Cache and cookies

> [!abstract] In one line
> Saved bits of websites and apps that make them faster but can get stale or broken.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Sticky notes on your monitor: handy until they're out of date and point you the wrong way.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **cache**: Saved copies of data kept to make things faster. Can go stale and cause odd problems.
> **cookie**: Small note a website stores in your browser, for example to remember that you're signed in.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- **Cache**: copies of pages and files so they load faster next time.
- **Cookies**: small notes websites keep, such as who you're signed in as.
- Stale cache or cookies cause broken pages and sign-in loops.
- A private window ignores them, which makes it a quick test.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> That site's cache and cookies (and check extensions).
