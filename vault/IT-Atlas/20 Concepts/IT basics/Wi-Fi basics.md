# Wi-Fi basics

> [!abstract] In one line
> Wireless networking: radio signals between your device and an access point.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Talking across a room: louder and closer is clearer; a crowd makes everyone repeat themselves.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SSID**: The name of a Wi-Fi network.
> **Wi-Fi**: Wireless network connection using radio signals.
> **certificate**: A digital ID card that proves a website or device is who it claims to be and enables encryption.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- The network name is the **SSID**.
- **2.4 GHz** reaches further but is slower and crowded; **5 GHz** and **6 GHz** are faster over shorter distances.
- Walls, distance, microwaves and many users all reduce speed.
- Office Wi-Fi often uses certificates or your work sign-in (802.1X) instead of a shared password.
