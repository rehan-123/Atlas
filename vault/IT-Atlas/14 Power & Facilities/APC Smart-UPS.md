# APC Smart-UPS

> [!abstract] In plain words
> APC's UPS units: a battery that keeps servers running in a power cut and can shut them down safely.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A UPS is a big battery that keeps equipment running during a power cut and shuts it down safely if the battery runs out.

**Made by:** [[Schneider Electric]] · **Runs:** Hardware · **Category:** [[Power & Facilities]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **UPS**: Uninterruptible power supply: a big battery that keeps equipment running in a power cut.
> **SNMP**: A protocol monitoring tools use to read status from network devices.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

APC by Schneider Electric UPS units with Network Management Cards for SNMP, email alerts and PowerChute-triggered graceful shutdowns. APC also makes PDUs and NetBotz environmental monitors.

## Main parts

- Network Management Card (web, SNMP)
- PowerChute Network Shutdown
- Battery packs (replace every few years)
- Switched and metered PDUs

## Alerts you'll see

- On battery
- Low battery or runtime
- Battery replacement needed (self-test failed)
- Overload
- NMC unreachable
