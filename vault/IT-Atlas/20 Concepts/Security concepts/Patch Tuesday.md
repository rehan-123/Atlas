# Patch Tuesday

> [!abstract] In one line
> Microsoft releases security updates on the second Tuesday of each month.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Microsoft's monthly update day: the second Tuesday of every month.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MSP**: Managed service provider: a company that runs IT for other companies.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Out-of-band releases happen for actively exploited bugs.
- Typical MSP rhythm: pilot ring within days, broad rollout within one to two weeks, faster for exploited zero-days.
