# Eaton UPS

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Eaton's UPS units and their shutdown software.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A UPS is a big battery that keeps equipment running during a power cut and shuts it down safely if the battery runs out.

**Made by:** [[Eaton]] · **Runs:** Hardware · **Category:** [[Power & Facilities]]

## In more detail

Eaton UPS units with Network-M2 or M3 cards and Intelligent Power Manager for monitoring and automated VM or host shutdown.
