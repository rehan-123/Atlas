# Microsoft Teams Rooms

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Meeting room systems that run Teams: a console on the table, cameras and a screen, signed in with a room account.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Business phone systems that run over the network or the internet.

**Made by:** [[Microsoft]] · **Category:** [[Voice & UC]]

## In more detail

Certified room systems (Windows or Android) using a resource account; managed in the Teams Rooms Pro portal and Teams admin center.

## When it breaks: what to check first

| Problem | Check this first |
|---|---|
| Room shows signed out | Check the resource account password hasn't expired and its licence is assigned |
