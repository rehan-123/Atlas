# Internet & Wi-Fi problems

> [!abstract] In plain words
> No internet, Wi-Fi trouble, websites, shared drives and VPN.

Part of [[Help Desk]].

| Problem | What's going on |
|---|---|
| [[No internet]] | Websites won't load and apps say you're offline. |
| [[Wi-Fi won't connect]] | The laptop can't join the Wi-Fi, or joins but says 'No internet, secured'. |
| [[Wi-Fi slow or drops]] | Wi-Fi is connected but slow, or keeps disconnecting, especially on calls. |
| [[Website won't load]] | One website won't open, but others work. |
| [[Can't open a shared drive]] | A mapped drive (like S: or H:) has a red X, or a network folder says access denied. |
| [[VPN won't connect]] | The VPN app fails to connect, or connects but nothing at the office opens. |
| [[Remote Desktop won't connect]] | Remote Desktop to a work PC or server fails. |
| [[Slow internet when working from home]] | Calls drop and files load slowly at home. |
