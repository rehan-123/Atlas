# Files, folders and drives

> [!abstract] In one line
> Where things are saved: on this PC, on a network share, or in the cloud.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Your desk drawer (C:), the shared office cabinet (network drive) and a storage unit you can reach from anywhere (OneDrive).

## How it works

- **C:** is the PC's own disk. Files here are lost if the laptop dies (unless backed up or synced).
- **Mapped drives** (S:, H:) point to folders on a file server.
- **OneDrive and SharePoint** keep files in Microsoft 365 and sync them to the PC.
- A file path like `C:\Users\NAME\Documents\report.docx` is its address.

## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- A user saved everything to the Desktop and the laptop was stolen. Are the files gone?
> Only if the Desktop wasn't synced to OneDrive (Known Folder Move) or backed up.
