# FileVault

> [!abstract] In plain words
> The Mac's built-in disk encryption.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Disk encryption scrambles everything on a laptop's drive, so a lost or stolen device doesn't leak data.

**Made by:** [[Apple]] · **Runs:** Built into macOS · **Category:** [[Security]] › [[Encryption]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **MDM**: Mobile device management: enrolling and controlling phones and laptops.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## In more detail

macOS full-disk encryption. Escrow the personal recovery key to your MDM.
