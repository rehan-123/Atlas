# Why restarting works

> [!abstract] In one line
> A restart clears memory, stops stuck programs and finishes updates.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Clearing the whole desk at the end of the day so tomorrow starts tidy.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **RAM**: Memory: the computer's short-term workspace for open apps. Cleared when you restart.
> **driver**: Small program that lets the operating system talk to a piece of hardware, like a printer or graphics card.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- RAM is wiped, so leaks and stuck processes disappear.
- Pending updates finish installing.
- Network connections and drivers reload.
- Closing the laptop lid or 'Shut down' with Fast Startup doesn't fully reset Windows; use **Restart**.
- If a restart only fixes it for a day, there's an underlying cause to find.
