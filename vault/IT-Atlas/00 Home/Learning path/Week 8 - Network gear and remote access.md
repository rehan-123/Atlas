## Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then click to reveal.

> [!question]- A whole site goes offline. What do you check first?
> Whether failover worked, the ISP status, and power (UPS alerts).

> [!question]- Why should RDP never be open to the internet?
> It gets brute-forced and exploited. Use a VPN, RD Gateway or ZTNA.
