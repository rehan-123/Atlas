# 3-2-1-1-0 rule

> [!abstract] In one line
> The modern backup rule of thumb.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Don't keep all your eggs in one basket, and keep one basket locked in a safe.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **immutable**: Can't be changed or deleted until a set date, even by an admin.
> **ransomware**: Malware that encrypts files and demands payment to unlock them.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- **3** copies of data (production plus two backups)
- **2** different media or storage types
- **1** copy offsite
- **1** copy offline, air-gapped or immutable
- **0** errors after recovery verification

Most ransomware recoveries that fail do so because the "1 immutable" was missing: attackers deleted the backups first.

> [!question]- Which part of the rule most often saves a company from ransomware?
