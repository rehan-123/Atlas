# Change your password

> [!abstract] What's going on
> A user's password is about to expire or they want to change it.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Changing the locks: the new key must be updated everywhere you keep a copy.

**Who can fix it:** Anyone can do the first steps; a tech does the rest. · **Area:** [[Sign-in & password problems]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **VPN**: An encrypted tunnel over the internet to a private network.
> **SSPR**: Self-service password reset: users reset their own password after proving who they are.
> **escalate**: Hand a problem to a more senior person or team, with your notes.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## Try this first

Anyone can do these, and they fix most cases.

1. Windows: Ctrl+Alt+Del > Change a password while on the company network or VPN.
2. Microsoft 365: mysignins.microsoft.com > Password (if self-service is set up).
3. Then update the password on your phone and any other device.

## If you're the tech

Words in CAPITALS are placeholders: replace them with your own values.

- Remote users must be on VPN to change a domain password, or have password writeback with SSPR.
- Check the new password meets length and history rules.

## Common causes and fixes

| Cause | Fix |
|---|---|
| Change rejected | Doesn't meet the policy; use a longer passphrase. |
| Old password still prompted | Update saved credentials on phone and PC. |

## Escalate when

- Repeated lockouts after a change

## Write it up
