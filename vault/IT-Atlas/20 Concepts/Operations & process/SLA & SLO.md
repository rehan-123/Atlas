# SLA & SLO

> [!abstract] In one line
> Service level agreement (the contract) and service level objective (the internal target).

> [!tip] Think of it like
> The SLA is the promise to the client; the SLO is the stricter internal target that keeps you safely inside it.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SLA**: Service level agreement: the response and fix times promised to a client. (On firewalls, an SD-WAN 'SLA' is a link-quality check.)
> **PSA**: Professional services automation: the MSP's ticketing, time and billing system.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Common SLAs: response time per priority, resolution targets, uptime.
- Set internal SLOs tighter than the SLA to leave margin.
- PSAs track SLA clocks; pausing them requires a legitimate status such as waiting on the customer.
