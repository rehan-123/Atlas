# Avanan

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Email security that connects directly to Microsoft 365 or Gmail and scans mail inside the mailbox.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** Email security filters catch phishing, malware and fake senders before the mail reaches the user.

**Made by:** [[Check Point]] · **Runs:** SaaS (API-based) · **Category:** [[Security]] › [[Email security]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **SaaS**: Software as a service: apps you use online, such as Microsoft 365.
> **phishing**: Fake messages designed to trick people into clicking or giving away passwords.
> **malware**: Any harmful software: viruses, ransomware, spyware and the like.

## In more detail

API-based email security that scans mail inside Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace after native filtering. Owned by Check Point.
