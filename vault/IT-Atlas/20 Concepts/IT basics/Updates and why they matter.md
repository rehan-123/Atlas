# Updates and why they matter

> [!abstract] In one line
> Updates fix security holes and bugs; skipping them leaves known doors open.

> [!tip] Think of it like
> Recalls on a car: the manufacturer found a fault and the fix only works if you take the car in.

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **RMM**: Remote monitoring and management: the MSP's main tool for watching and fixing client machines.
> **browser**: The app you use to open websites: Edge, Chrome, Safari or Firefox.
> *More in the [[Glossary]].*

## How it works

- Windows has monthly security updates (Patch Tuesday, second Tuesday of the month).
- Apps like browsers, Office, Zoom and Adobe need updates too.
- Updates usually need a restart to finish.
- Companies test and roll out updates in waves using the RMM, Intune or WSUS.
