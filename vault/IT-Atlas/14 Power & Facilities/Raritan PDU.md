# Raritan PDU

> [!abstract] In plain words
> Smart power strips for racks that measure power use and can switch individual outlets off and on remotely.
>
> **What kind of tool is this?** A UPS is a big battery that keeps equipment running during a power cut and shuts it down safely if the battery runs out.

**Made by:** [[Legrand]] · **Category:** [[Power & Facilities]]

> [!info]+ Words on this page
> **UPS**: Uninterruptible power supply: a big battery that keeps equipment running in a power cut.
> **SNMP**: A protocol monitoring tools use to read status from network devices.
> **port**: A numbered door on a device that a specific service listens on, for example 443 for HTTPS or 3389 for RDP.

## In more detail

Intelligent rack PDUs with per-outlet metering and switching, web UI, SNMP and environmental sensor ports.
